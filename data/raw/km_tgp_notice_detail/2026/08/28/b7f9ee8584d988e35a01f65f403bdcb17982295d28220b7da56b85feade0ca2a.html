EMERGENT REPAIR AT STA 32 EFFECTIVE 6-8-2026

 

Pursuant to Article XII of the General Terms and Conditions of Tennessee's FERC Gas Tariff, Tennessee is posting notice of an emergent repair issue at Station 32 near Jasper, TX.  Tennessee has discovered an issue at the station which necessitates repairs.   Tennessee estimates the current impact to be up to 350,000 Dths. Based upon current demand and nominations, Secondary Out of the Path nominations pathed through Segment 132 FH may be at risk as early as Timely Cycle for the gas day of Tuesday, June 9, 2026 and until further notice.  Previously scheduled volumes will not be curtailed for the current gas day of June 8, 2026; however, increases pathed through STA 32 (Segment 132 FH) for the remainder of the gas day are at risk. 

 

Updates will be posted as more information becomes available.

 

Please contact your Scheduler if you have any questions.
