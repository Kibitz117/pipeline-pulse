Subject:  MONTHLY STORAGE TRANSFER REMINDER
Notice Text:

MONTHLY STORAGE TRANSFERS FOR SEPTEMBER 1, 2026

DART provides the ability for storage customers to enter their own storage transfers online.  The transfers must be confirmed on or before the effective date.  If you have a terminated or recalled contract that needs the balance transferred to the base storage contract holder or a new contract, these transfers need to be completed on or before the first of the month.  

For terminated storage contracts per the firm storage rate schedule of the Tennessee Gas Pipeline tariff on First Revised Sheet No. 217, Section 11: “Shippers must withdraw or transfer its Storage Balance on or before the date of the expiration of its FS Service Agreement.  To the extent that a Shipper fails to dispose of or transfer its entire Storage Balance, Transporter shall retain and take title to Shipper's remaining Storage Balance at no cost to Transporter.”   

PLEASE NOTE:  When the first of the month falls on a weekend or holiday, any small volume left on a terminated or recalled storage contract will be moved for the customers by TGP to the new valid contract if requested by the customer on the first business day following the weekend or holiday provided that efforts have been made prior to the first and/or holiday to transfer allocated balances prior to the weekend and/or holiday before requesting TGP to move the remaining balance.

TENNESSEE GAS PIPELINE WILL CONFISCATE ANY REMAINING BALANCE NOT TRANSFERRED OR REQUESTED BY THE CUSTOMER TO BE TRANSFERRED ON TERMINATED FIRM STORAGE CONTRACTS.

 

Please contact your Primary Gas Scheduler if you need procedures and/or help with your storage transfer.
