EMERGENT REPAIR BETWEEN MLV 533-2 AND 534-2 EFFECTIVE 6-5-2026

 

Pursuant to Article XII of the General Terms and Conditions of Tennessee's FERC Gas Tariff, Tennessee is posting notice of an Emergent Repair between MLV 533-2 and MLV 534-2 near Purvis, Mississippi located in Stone County.  Tennessee has identified an issue that necessitates repairs.  The affected segment has been isolated.  Tennessee estimates the current impact to be up to 225,000 Dths.  Based on current nominations, Secondary Out of the Path nominations pathed through STA 534 South of the Pool (Segment 530 BH) could be at risk as early as Timely Cycle for the gas day of Saturday, June 6, 2026 and until further notice.  Previously scheduled nominations for the current gas day of June 5, 2026 will not be curtailed; however, increases pathed through STA 534 South of the Pool (Segment 530 BH) for the remainder of today's gas day are at risk.

 

The below meters will remain shut in during the outage:

412753  SKYEMS/TGP Pistol Ridge RIP 533 C-101.2

412758  PETROHRV/TGP Highway 13 RIP 533 D-101.2

412750  PRP INC/TGP Pistol Ridge RIP 533 B-101.2

 

Updates will be provided as more information becomes available.

 

Please contact your Account Representative or Scheduler if you have any questions.
