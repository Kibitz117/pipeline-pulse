EMERGENT REPAIR BETWEEN MLV 559-2A TO 560-2 EFFECTIVE 7-7-2026

 

Pursuant to Article XII of the General Terms and Conditions of Tennessee's FERC Gas Tariff, Tennessee is posting notice of an emergent repair issue at MLV 559-2A to 560-2 near Centerville, TN.  Tennessee has discovered an issue that necessitates repairs.  Tennessee will need to isolate this area for pipeline inspections. Tennessee is estimating the current impact to be up to 175,000 Dths.  Based upon current demand and nominations, Secondary Out of the Path nominations pathed through STA 860 South 500 Line (Segment 548 BH) may be at risk as early as Timely Cycle for the gas day of Wednesday, July 8, 2026 and until further notice.  Previously scheduled volumes will not be curtailed for the current gas day of June 7, 2026; however, increases pathed through STA 860 South 500 Line (Segment 548 BH) for the remainder of the gas day are at risk. 

 

Updates will be posted as more information becomes available.

 

Please contact your Scheduler if you have any questions.
