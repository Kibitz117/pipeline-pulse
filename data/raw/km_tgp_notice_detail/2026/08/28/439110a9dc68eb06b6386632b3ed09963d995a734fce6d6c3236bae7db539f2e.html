EMERGENT REPAIR BETWEEN MLV 113-2 AND MLV 114-2 (BROAD RUN LATERAL) EFFECTIVE 7-8-2026

 

Pursuant to Article XII of the General Terms and Conditions of Tennessee's FERC Gas Tariff, Tennessee is posting notice of an emergent repair between MLV 113-2 and MLV 114-2 near Catlettsburg, KY.  The line has been isolated.  Tennessee has personnel on site to investigate repairs.

Updates will be posted as more information becomes available.
