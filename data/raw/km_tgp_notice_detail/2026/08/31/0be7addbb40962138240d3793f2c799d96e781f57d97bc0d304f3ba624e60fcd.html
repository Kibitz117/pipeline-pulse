OFO DAILY CRITICAL DAY 1 EFFECTIVE 8-29-2026

 

Due to forecasted milder weather with associated lesser demand moving into the Northeast areas of its system, in order to protect the operational integrity of its system, effective for the Gas Day (“GD”) of Saturday, August 29, 2026 Tennessee Gas Pipeline Company, L.L.C. (“Tennessee”) is implementing an OFO Daily Critical Day (“CD”) 1 for all areas East of STA 219 for over-deliveries into the system or under-takes out of the system pursuant to Article X of the General Terms and Conditions of Tennessee's FERC NGA Gas Tariff.  Tennessee is lifting the OFO Daily Critical Day 1 for all areas East of STA 219 effective for the Gas Day of Monday, August 31, 2026 and until further notice.

 

This OFO will be effective for LMS-PA, SA contracts acting as balancing parties and LMS-MA Balancing Parties only.
